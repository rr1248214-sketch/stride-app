    How to read the accuracy result
    

      Absolute error = |detected − actual|. Percentage error = (|detected − actual| ÷ actual) × 100.
      Under about 5% is good for a browser-based counter. Anything above about 15% means the
      sensitivity setting needs adjusting for your phone and carrying position.
